Arca di Noah

An animal shelter in Sicily. As its Marketing & Communication Manager (August 2025 to February 2026, remote), I moved the account from informative posts to video-first storytelling.

In 90 days, with no paid ads, the account reached 545K people and its Reels were watched 1.1M times.

53 Reels between 17 November 2025 and 15 February 2026, with 12K shares and 2.6K saves. Followers grew 600% to 1,400, and inquiries went from almost none to 10 qualified adoption applications in one month.

Simple on purpose.

The audience was mostly Italian, with a lot of older followers and mums. They didn’t want fast cuts and effects. They wanted clear text on screen, a bit of humour and honest updates about the dogs.

So that’s what I made: videos built for the people who adopt, not to impress other editors.

Captions are in Italian; each piece has an English summary.

With AI

Image tools let me test a direction before anyone spends time or money on it. I write the brief and direct the generations until the result fits the audience. Everything here uses AI-generated imagery.

Winter blues, for students

An Instagram carousel from a health communication intervention at the University of Amsterdam, for students who feel low every winter but don’t think it’s serious enough to ask for help. It opens with a question, lets students recognise themselves, then removes the “depressed enough” barrier.

Sottosuono

Underground electronic music collective, co-founded in Bologna, now relaunching in Amsterdam.

Five nights so far, usually 60 to 100 people each. I curate the line-ups, play vinyl, and promote every event through Instagram, flyers, broadcasts and word of mouth.

This audience wants the opposite of the shelter’s: texture, mood, a bit of mystery. So the flyers use mirrored ink, like a Rorschach test, and ask the same question as the card at the top of this page.
